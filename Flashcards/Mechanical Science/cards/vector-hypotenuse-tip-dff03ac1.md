## When working with vectors and right triangles, which side corresponds to the magnitude of the vector?

---

The hypotenuse corresponds to the magnitude of the vector.

So in triangle relations: hypotenuse $=|\vec{A}|$, adjacent/opposite sides are the components $A_x$ and $A_y$.
