## What is the usual convention for measuring the angle $\theta$ of a vector in 2D?

---

Measure $\theta$ from the positive $x$-axis anticlockwise toward the vector.

This convention yields $\theta$ in the range $[0,360^\circ)$ (or $[0,2\pi)$ in radians) when using full-circle angles; use sign information of components to determine quadrant.
