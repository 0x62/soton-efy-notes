## What is a unit vector and what are $i$ and $j$?

---

$$|i|=|j|=1$$

A unit vector has magnitude 1 and indicates direction. In 2D Cartesian coordinates, $i$ and $j$ are the unit vectors along the $x$- and $y$-axes respectively.
