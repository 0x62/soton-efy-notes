## How do you compute the magnitude $|A|$ of a 2D vector from its components $A_x$ and $A_y$?

---

$$|A|=\sqrt{A_{x}^2+A_{y}^2}$$

Derivation:

By Pythagoras on the right triangle with legs $A_x$ and $A_y$ and hypotenuse $|A|$:

$$A_x^2+A_y^2=|A|^2\Rightarrow |A|=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2}$$
