## Express shear strain in terms of the angle $\gamma$ and displacement $\Delta x$ over height $L$.

---

$$\tan\gamma=\frac{\Delta x}{L}$$

For small angles $\gamma$ (in radians) $\tan\gamma\approx\gamma$, so shear strain is often written $\gamma\approx\dfrac{\Delta x}{L}$.
