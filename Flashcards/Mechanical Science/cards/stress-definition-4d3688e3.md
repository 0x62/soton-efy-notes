## Define stress and give the formula for normal (tensile/compressive) stress $\sigma$.

---

$$\sigma=\frac{F}{A}$$

Stress is force per unit area (intensity of internal force). Units: N/m² = Pa. The same form applies to shear stress $\tau$ and pressure $P$ (bulk stress) when interpreted appropriately.
