## How is a 2D vector $A$ expressed using unit vectors $i$ and $j$?

---

$$A=A_x\,i + A_y\,j$$

Here $A_x$ and $A_y$ are the scalar components along the $x$ and $y$ axes, and $i$, $j$ are the corresponding unit vectors.
