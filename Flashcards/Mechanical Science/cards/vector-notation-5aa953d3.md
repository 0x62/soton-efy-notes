## How can the vector from origin to point P be notated and how are its angle and magnitude denoted?

---

$$\text{Vector: }\overrightarrow{OP}=A=\vec{A}=\widehat{A}=\underline{A}$$

$$\text{Angle: }\angle\overrightarrow{OP}=\theta$$

$$\text{Magnitude: }|OP|$$

Multiple notations are used in textbooks: arrows, bold, hats or underlines for vectors; vertical bars for magnitude; the angle symbol for direction.
