## What is the effective spring constant $k_{\text{eff}}$ for springs in parallel?

---

$$k_{\text{eff}}=k_1+k_2+k_3+\dots$$

For $n$ identical springs each of stiffness $k$, $$k_{\text{eff}}=n\,k\,.$$

Reason: each spring experiences the same extension and the total force is the sum of individual forces, so stiffnesses add directly.
