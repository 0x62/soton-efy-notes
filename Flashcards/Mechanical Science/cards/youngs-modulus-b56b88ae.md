## State Young's modulus $E$ and its relation to stress $\sigma$ and strain $\epsilon$.

---

$$E=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}$$

Expanded: $$E=\frac{F/A}{\Delta x / L}=\frac{F\,L}{A\,\Delta x}\,.$$

Units: N/m² = Pa. $E$ measures stiffness in tension/compression.
