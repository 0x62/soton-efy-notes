## Define tensile (normal) strain $\epsilon$ in terms of change in length $\Delta x$ and original length $L$

---

$$\epsilon=\frac{\Delta x}{L}$$

Strain is dimensionless (ratio). Positive for extension, negative for compression. Used in Young's modulus relations.
