## What is ultimate tensile strength (UTS)?

---

UTS is the maximum tensile stress a material can withstand before breaking.

UTS is determined experimentally and occurs beyond the elastic limit (after yielding/plastic deformation begins). On a stress–strain curve it is the peak stress prior to failure.
