## Define bulk modulus $B$ and relate it to pressure $P$ and strain $\epsilon_v$

---

$$B=\frac{P}{\epsilon_v}$$

That is, bulk modulus equals pressure (bulk stress) divided by fractional volume change. Units: N/m² = Pa.
