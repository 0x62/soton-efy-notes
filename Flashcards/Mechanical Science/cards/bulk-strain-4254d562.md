## Define bulk strain $\epsilon_v$ in terms of volume change $\Delta V$ and original volume $V$

---

$$\epsilon_v=\frac{\Delta V}{V}$$

Bulk strain is the fractional change in volume under uniform (hydrostatic) stress.
