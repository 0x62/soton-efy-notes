## What is the effective spring constant $k_{\text{eff}}$ for springs in series?

---

$$\frac{1}{k_{\text{eff}}}=\frac{1}{k_1}+\frac{1}{k_2}+\frac{1}{k_3}+\dots$$

For two springs in series this simplifies to: $$k_{\text{eff}}=\frac{k_1\,k_2}{k_1+k_2}\,.$$

Reason: the same force acts on each spring and total extension is the sum of individual extensions, so reciprocal stiffnesses add.
