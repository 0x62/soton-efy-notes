## Express the spring constant $k$ in terms of Young's modulus $E$, cross-sectional area $A$ and length $L$.

---

$$k=\frac{EA}{L}$$

Derivation: equate Hooke's law $F=k\Delta x$ with the Young's-modulus form $F=\dfrac{EA}{L}\,\Delta x$. Cancelling $\Delta x$ gives $k=\dfrac{EA}{L}$.
