## Given a vector with magnitude $|A|$ and angle $\theta$ (measured from the $x$-axis), what are its components $A_x$ and $A_y$?

---

$$A_{x}=|A|\cos\theta \qquad A_{y}=|A|\sin\theta$$

Derivation:

Use the right triangle formed by the vector as the hypotenuse. Adjacent side = $|A|\cos\theta$, opposite side = $|A|\sin\theta$ by the definitions of cosine and sine.
