## What are the definitions of $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$ and $\tan\theta$ in a right triangle?

---

$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

When working with vectors, the vector acts as the hypotenuse and its components are the adjacent/opposite sides.
