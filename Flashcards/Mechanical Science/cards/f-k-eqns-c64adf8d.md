## Show the two expressions for axial force $F$ using Hooke's law and Young's-modulus form, and derive $k$.

---

$$F=k\,\Delta x\qquad F=\frac{EA}{L}\,\Delta x$$

Equate the two: $k\,\Delta x=\dfrac{EA}{L}\,\Delta x$, cancel $\Delta x$ to get $$k=\frac{EA}{L}\,.$$
