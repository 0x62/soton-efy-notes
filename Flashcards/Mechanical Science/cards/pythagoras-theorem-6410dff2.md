## What is Pythagoras' theorem and its rearranged form for the hypotenuse $h$?

---

$$a^2+b^2=h^2$$

$$h=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$$

Used to relate the side lengths of a right triangle; fundamental for finding vector magnitudes from components.
