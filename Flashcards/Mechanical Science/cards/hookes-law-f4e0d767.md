## State Hooke's Law relating force $F$, spring constant $k$ and extension $x$.

---

$$F = k\,x$$

Hooke's Law applies only within the elastic limit of a spring or solid body. From this you can also write: $$x=\frac{F}{k}\qquad k=\frac{F}{x}\,.$$
