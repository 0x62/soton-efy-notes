## Write the formulas for tensile/compressive stress $\sigma$, shear stress $\tau$ and bulk stress (pressure) $P$

---

$$\sigma=\frac{F}{A}\qquad \tau=\frac{F}{A}\qquad P=\frac{F}{A}$$

All three are intensities of force per unit area; they differ by the direction of force relative to the surface (normal vs tangent vs uniform/hydrostatic).
