## Given a rod of length $L$, area $A$, Young's modulus $E$ and axial force $F$, what is the extension $\Delta x$?

---

$$\Delta x=\frac{F\,L}{A\,E}$$

Derivation: start from $\sigma=\epsilon E$, substitute $\dfrac{F}{A}=E\dfrac{\Delta x}{L}$ and solve for $\Delta x$ to obtain $\Delta x=\dfrac{F L}{A E}$.
