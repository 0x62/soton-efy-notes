## What is the relation between vectors $\overrightarrow{OP}$ and $\overrightarrow{PO}$ and how do their magnitudes compare?

---

$$\overrightarrow{PO}=-\overrightarrow{OP}\qquad |\overrightarrow{PO}|=|\overrightarrow{OP}|$$

Explanation:

Reversing the direction of a vector multiplies it by $-1$; the magnitude (length) remains the same while the direction is opposite.
