## Define shear modulus $G$ and show its relation to shear stress $\tau$ and shear strain $\gamma$

---

$$G=\frac{\tau}{\gamma}$$

More generally $G=\dfrac{\tau}{\tan\gamma}$; for small $\gamma$ (radians) $\tan\gamma\approx\gamma$ so $G\approx\dfrac{\tau}{\gamma}$. Units: N/m² = Pa.
