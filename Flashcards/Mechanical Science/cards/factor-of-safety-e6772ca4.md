## Give the formula for factor of safety (FoS) in terms of UTS and maximum allowed stress

---

$$\text{FoS}=\frac{\text{UTS}}{\text{Max allowed stress}}$$

FoS is used in design to ensure components operate well below failure stress; choose FoS based on material behavior (brittle vs ductile) and application.
