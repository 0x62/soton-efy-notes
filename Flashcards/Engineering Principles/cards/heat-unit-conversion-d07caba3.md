## What is the relation between the calorie, the Btu and the joule?

---

$$1\ \text{cal} = 3.968\times10^{-3}\ \text{Btu} = 4.1867\ \text{J}$$

Note: the dietary "Calorie" is actually a kilocalorie (1000 cal). The joule is the SI unit of heat/energy; the given conversion for the calorie is exact by definition in this note.
