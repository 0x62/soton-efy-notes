## What is the formula for linear thermal expansion of a length $L$ when temperature changes by $\Delta T$?

---

$$\Delta L = L\,\alpha\,\Delta T$$

Here $\alpha$ is the coefficient of linear expansion (units $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ or per degree). It is material dependent and often treated as constant over moderate temperature ranges.
