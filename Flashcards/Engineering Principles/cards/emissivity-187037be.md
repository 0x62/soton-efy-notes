## What is emissivity $\epsilon$ and what are its limits and meaning?

---

Emissivity $\epsilon$ is a dimensionless factor $0\le\epsilon\le1$ that scales actual radiated power relative to an ideal blackbody ($\epsilon=1$).

$\epsilon=1$ denotes a perfect blackbody that absorbs and emits all incident radiation; real surfaces have $\epsilon<1$ depending on material and finish.
