## Write the conduction heat-transfer rate formula $P_{cond}=\frac{Q}{t}=\dots$ and define each symbol in the formula.

---

$$P_{cond}=\frac{Q}{t}=k\,A\frac{T_H-T_C}{L}$$

$P_{cond}$ is heat flow rate (power, e.g. W), $Q$ is heat transferred in time $t$, $k$ is thermal conductivity (units W/(m\,K)), $A$ is cross-sectional area, $L$ is slab thickness (distance between faces), and $T_H,T_C$ are temperatures of the hot and cold faces respectively.
