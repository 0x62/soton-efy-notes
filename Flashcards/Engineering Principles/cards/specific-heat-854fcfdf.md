## What is the specific heat formula and what does each symbol mean in $Q=cm\Delta T$?

---

$$Q = c\,m\,\Delta T$$

$c$ is the specific heat (heat capacity per unit mass, units J/(kg\,K) or cal/(g\,°C)). $m$ is mass and $\Delta T=T_f-T_i$. For water: $c=1\ \text{cal}/\text{g}\cdot\,\!\!\,\!\,^{\circ}\text{C}=4186\ \text{J}/\text{kg}\cdot\text{K}$ (approx.).
