## What is the heat capacity relation between heat $Q$ and temperature change $\Delta T$ for an object?

---

$$Q = C\,\Delta T$$

$C$ is the heat capacity of the object (units energy per kelvin, e.g. J/K). $T_i$ and $T_f$ can be used via $\Delta T=T_f-T_i$. $C$ depends on the amount of material and its composition.
