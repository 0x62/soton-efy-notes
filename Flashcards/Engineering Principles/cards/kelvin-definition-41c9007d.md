## How is temperature $T$ (in kelvins) defined using a constant-volume gas thermometer relative to the triple point of water?

---

$$T = 273.16\ \mathrm{K} \times \left(\lim_{\text{gas}\to 0} \frac{p}{p_3}\right)$$

Here $p$ is the gas pressure measured at the temperature of interest and $p_3$ is the gas pressure at the triple point of water ($273.16\ \mathrm{K}$). The limit gas\to0 indicates extrapolation to zero gas density to remove nonideal-gas effects.
