## What is the formula for volume expansion and how does it relate to linear expansion ($\alpha$)?

---

$$\Delta V = V\,\beta\,\Delta T\\beta = 3\alpha$$

$\beta$ is the coefficient of volume expansion (units $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$). For isotropic solids undergoing small expansions, $\beta\approx3\alpha$ because volume change is approximately the sum of three orthogonal linear expansions.
