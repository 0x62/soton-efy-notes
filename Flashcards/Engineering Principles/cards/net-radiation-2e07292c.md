## What is the net radiative heat transfer between an object at temperature $T$ and an environment at temperature $T_{env}$?

---

$$P_{net}=\sigma\,\epsilon\,A\,(T_{env}^4-T^4)$$

$P_{net}>0$ means net energy is absorbed from the environment; $P_{net}<0$ means the object is losing net energy by radiation. Both object and environment emission/absorption are accounted for by the $T^4$ terms.
