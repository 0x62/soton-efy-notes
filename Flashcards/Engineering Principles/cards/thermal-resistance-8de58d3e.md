## What is the thermal resistance (R-value) of a slab and how is it related to thermal conductivity $k$?

---

$$R = \frac{L}{k}$$

$R$ is the resistance to heat conduction for a slab of thickness $L$ and conductivity $k$. Larger $R$ means better insulation. Note $R$ is defined for a specified thickness, not as a pure material property.
