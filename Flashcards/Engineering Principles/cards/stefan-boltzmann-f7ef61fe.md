## What is the Stefan–Boltzmann law for power radiated by a surface and the value of $\sigma$?

---

$$P_{rad}=\sigma\,\epsilon\,A\,T^4$$

$\sigma=5.6704\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}$ is the Stefan–Boltzmann constant, $\epsilon$ is emissivity (dimensionless, $0\le\epsilon\le1$), $A$ surface area, and $T$ temperature in kelvins. This gives the emitted electromagnetic power from the surface.
