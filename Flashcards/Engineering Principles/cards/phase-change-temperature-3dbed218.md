## What happens to the temperature of a sample while it undergoes a phase change even though heat is being added or removed?

---

The temperature remains constant while the phase change occurs, even as heat is added or removed.

Added heat goes into changing microscopic binding/structure (latent heat $L$) rather than increasing kinetic energy, so $T$ does not change until the phase change completes.
