## State the Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics.

---

When two bodies $A$ and $B$ are each in thermal equilibrium with a third body $T$, then $A$ and $B$ are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

This establishes temperature as a transitive property: bodies in thermal equilibrium share the same temperature, which justifies using thermometers to compare temperatures without direct contact between measured bodies.
