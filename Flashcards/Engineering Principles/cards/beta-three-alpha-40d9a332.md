## Why is $\beta=3\alpha$ for a solid (brief derivation)?

---

Final: $$\beta = 3\alpha$$

Derivation: For small fractional linear expansion in each orthogonal direction $\Delta L/L=\alpha\Delta T$. The fractional volume change is

$$\frac{\Delta V}{V}=(1+\alpha\Delta T)^3-1\approx 3\alpha\Delta T$$

neglecting second-order terms, so $\beta=3\alpha$.
