## What is the heat required for a phase change and what are the values for water's latent heats ($L_V$, $L_F$)?

---

$$Q = L\,m$$

For vaporisation of water at its normal boiling point: $$L_V = 539\ \text{cal/g} = 2256\ \text{kJ/kg}.$$ For fusion (melting) of ice: $$L_F = 79.5\ \text{cal/g} = 333\ \text{kJ/kg}.$$

$L$ is the heat of transformation per unit mass; during a phase change the temperature remains constant while heat $Q$ = $L m$ is absorbed or released.
