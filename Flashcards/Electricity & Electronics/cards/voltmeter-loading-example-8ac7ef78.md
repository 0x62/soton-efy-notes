## What is the effective resistance when measuring across a $1\,\mathrm{M}\Omega$ resistor with a $10\,\mathrm{M}\Omega$ voltmeter?

---

$$R_{eff}=\dfrac{1\times10}{1+10}\,\mathrm{M\Omega}=0.91\,\mathrm{M\Omega}$$

Using parallel resistor formula $$R_{eff}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$$ with $R_1=1\,\mathrm{M}\Omega$ and $R_2=10\,\mathrm{M}\Omega$ gives $0.909\ldots\approx0.91\,\mathrm{M}\Omega$.
