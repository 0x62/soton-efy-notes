## What is the efficiency of power transfer at the maximum power transfer condition $R_l=R_s$?

---

Efficiency = 50%.

When $R_l=R_s$, the source and load dissipate equal power, so half the power generated by the source is delivered to the load and half is lost in the source resistance.
