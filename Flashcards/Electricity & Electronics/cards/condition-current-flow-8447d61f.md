## When does electric current flow through a component in a circuit?

---

Current flows only when there is a potential difference across the component (voltages at its terminals differ).

If both terminals are at the same potential (no voltage difference), the net current through that component is zero (ignoring transient or non-ideal effects). Example: two equal-voltage nodes connected by a resistor let no steady current flow.
