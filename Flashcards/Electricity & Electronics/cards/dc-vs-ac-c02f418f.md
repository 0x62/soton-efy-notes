## What is the difference between Direct Current (DC) and Alternating Current (AC)? Include typical UK mains values.

---

DC flows in one direction and provides a steady voltage; AC periodically reverses direction and voltage.

UK mains: voltage alternates between $-230\,\mathrm{V}$ and $+230\,\mathrm{V}$ at $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Many devices convert AC to DC for electronics.
