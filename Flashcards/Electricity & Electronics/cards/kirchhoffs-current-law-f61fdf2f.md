## State Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) and give the algebraic form for a junction with currents $I_1,I_2,I_3$.

---

The total current entering a junction equals the total current leaving it; algebraically the signed sum is zero.

Example: if $I_1$ enters and $I_2,I_3$ leave then

$$I_1+(-I_2)+(-I_3)=0$$

Equivalently $I_{\text{entering}}=I_{\text{leaving}}$.
