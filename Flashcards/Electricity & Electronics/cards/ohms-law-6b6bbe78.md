## What is Ohm's law?

---

$$V = IR$$

- $R$ = resistance (ohms, $\Omega$)
- $V$ = voltage (potential difference) across the resistor (volts, V)
- $I$ = current through the resistor (amperes, A)

Unit: $1\ \Omega = 1\ \mathrm{V\ A^{-1}}$. Ohm's law applies to ohmic materials where $V\propto I$.
