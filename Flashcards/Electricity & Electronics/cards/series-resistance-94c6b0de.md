## Total resistance $R_{t}$ for resistors in series $R_1,R_2,R_3,\dots$?

---

$$R_{t}=R_{1}+R_{2}+R_{3}+\dots$$

In series the same current flows through every resistor. Voltage drops add: $$V_{s}=V_{R1}+V_{R2}+\dots=I(R_{1}+R_{2}+\dots).$$
