## What is the balance condition for a Wheatstone Bridge and the formula for an unknown resistor $R_x$ when $R_s$ is the adjustable resistor?

---

Balance condition: $$\dfrac{R_1}{R_3}=\dfrac{R_2}{R_4}$$

If $R_x$ is $R_1$ and $R_3$ is the adjustable resistor $R_s$, then

$R_x = R_s\times\dfrac{R_2}{R_4}$$

At balance the bridge output voltage $V_{out}=0$ and voltage ratios across the two dividers are equal.
