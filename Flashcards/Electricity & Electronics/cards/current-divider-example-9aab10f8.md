## Given $R_{1}=50\,\Omega$, $R_{2}=100\,\Omega$ and total source current $I_{t}=20\,\mathrm{A}$, find $I_{R1}$ and $I_{R2}$.

---

$$I_{R1}=13.33\,\mathrm{A}\quad I_{R2}=6.67\,\mathrm{A}$$

Using current-divider: $I_{R1}=I_{t}\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}=20\times\dfrac{100}{150}=13.33\,\mathrm{A}$. Then $I_{R2}=I_{t}-I_{R1}=6.67\,\mathrm{A}$. Check: $13.33+6.67\approx20\,\mathrm{A}$.
