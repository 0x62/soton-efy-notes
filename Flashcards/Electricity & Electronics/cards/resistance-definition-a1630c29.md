## Define electrical resistance and its SI unit; give formulas for $R$ in terms of $V$, $I$, and $P$.

---

Resistance $R$ opposes current flow. Unit: ohm ($\Omega$) with $1\ \Omega=\dfrac{1\ V}{1\ A}$. 

Formulas:

$$R=\dfrac{V}{I}$$

$$R=\dfrac{P}{I^2}$$

$$R=\dfrac{V^2}{P}$$

These follow from Ohm's Law and power relations: $R=V/I$, and substituting $P=I^2R$ or $P=V^2/R$ then rearranging.
