## Define electric current and its SI unit.

---

Electric current is the flow of electric charge (typically electrons) through a circuit. Unit: ampere ($A$), where $1\ A = 1\ C\,s^{-1}$.

Current is usually denoted by $I$. One ampere means one coulomb of charge passes a point each second.
