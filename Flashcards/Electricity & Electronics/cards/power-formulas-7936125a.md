## What are the common electrical power formulas?

---

$$P=VI$$

$$P=I^{2}R$$

$$P=\dfrac{V^{2}}{R}$$

Use $P=I^{2}R$ when resistance is fixed (e.g. heaters). In transmission lines losses are estimated with $P=I^{2}R$ to show why reducing current (higher voltage) is efficient.
