## What is internal resistance and how does it affect output voltage?

---

The internal resistance $r$ is the source's inherent resistance; it causes a voltage drop $Ir$, so the terminal voltage is $$V = EMF - Ir$$

As current $I$ increases, more voltage is lost across $r$, reducing the usable terminal voltage.
