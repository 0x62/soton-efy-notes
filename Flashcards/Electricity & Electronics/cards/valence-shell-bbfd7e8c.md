## What is the valence and the valence shell of an atom?

---

Valence shell = outermost electron shell; Valence = number of electrons in that shell.

The valence shell determines how an atom interacts chemically and electrically. Atoms with loosely bound valence electrons (large valence) are more likely to conduct electricity; atoms with tightly bound valence electrons act as insulators.
