## Given energy $E$ and voltage $V$, what is the charge $Q$?

---

$Q=\dfrac{E}{V}$

From $V=E/Q$ rearranged. If $E$ joules of energy are used across a potential difference $V$, the amount of charge moved is $E/V$.
