## State the maximum power transfer theorem for a DC source.

---

Maximum power is delivered to the load when the load resistance equals the source (Thevenin) resistance: $$R_l = R_s$$

At this condition the load receives its maximum possible power for that source; practical caveat: efficiency is only 50% because equal power is dissipated in $R_s$.
