## What is the equivalent resistance for two resistors $R_1$ and $R_2$ in parallel?

$R_p = \frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$

---

$$R_p = \frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$$

Derivation:

$$\frac{1}{R_p}=\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_2}=\frac{R_1+R_2}{R_1R_2}\quad\Rightarrow\quad R_p=\frac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}\,.$$
