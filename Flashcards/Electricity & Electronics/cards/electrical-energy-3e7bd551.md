## What is electrical energy $W$ in terms of voltage, current and time?

---

$$W = V I t$$

Energy $W$ equals power $P=VI$ multiplied by time $t$, so $W=P t=VI t$. The note's expression $W=V\times l\times t$ likely uses a lowercase $l$ as a typographic substitution for current $I$; the correct symbol for current is $I$.

- $W$ = energy (joules, J)
- $V$ = voltage (V)
- $I$ = current (A)
- $t$ = time (s)

Units check: $\mathrm{V\cdot A\cdot s}=\mathrm{J}$.
