## What is electrical current in terms of charge and time?

---

$$I = \frac{Q}{t}$$

Electrical current $I$ is the charge $Q$ transported per unit time $t$.

- $Q$ = charge (coulombs, C)
- $t$ = time (seconds, s)
- SI unit: ampere (A), where $1\ \mathrm{A}=1\ \mathrm{C\ s^{-1}}$
