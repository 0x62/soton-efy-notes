## What is the relation $R = \dfrac{\rho \ell}{a}$ and what do the symbols represent?

---

$$R = \frac{\rho \ell}{a}$$

- $R$ = resistance (\Omega)
- $\rho$ = resistivity of the material (\Omega\cdot m)
- $\ell$ = length of the conductor (m)
- $a$ = cross-sectional area (m$^2$)

Resistance scales linearly with length and inversely with area; $\rho$ is a material property (temperature dependent).
