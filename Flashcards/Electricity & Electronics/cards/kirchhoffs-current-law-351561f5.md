## State Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL).

---

$$I_{t}=I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}+\dots$$

KCL: the algebraic sum of currents entering a node equals the sum leaving the node. This expresses conservation of charge at circuit junctions.
