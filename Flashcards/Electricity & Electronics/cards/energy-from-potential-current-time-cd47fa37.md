## Given voltage $V$, current $I$ and time $t$, what is the energy $E$?

---

$E=V\times I\times t$

Derivation:

$$P=V\,I$$

$$E=P\,t$$

Substitute $P$: $$E=(V\,I)\,t=V\,I\,t$$

This gives energy (joules) as power times time, or equivalently voltage times current times time.
