## Current through $R_{1}$ in two parallel resistors $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ given total source current $I_{t}$.

---

$$I_{R1}=I_{t}\times\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$$

Derivation: $I_{t}=I_{R1}+I_{R2}$ with $I_{R1}=\dfrac{V}{R_{1}}$, $I_{R2}=\dfrac{V}{R_{2}}$. Solve $V=\dfrac{I_{t}}{\frac{1}{R_{1}}+\frac{1}{R_{2}}}$ and substitute into $I_{R1}=\dfrac{V}{R_{1}}$ to get $I_{R1}=I_{t}\times\dfrac{\frac{1}{R_{1}}}{\frac{1}{R_{1}}+\frac{1}{R_{2}}}=I_{t}\times\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$.
