## For a source with EMF $V$, source resistance $R_s$ and load resistance $R_l$, what is the circuit current $I$?

---

$$I = \dfrac{V}{R_s + R_l}$$

This follows from Ohm's law applied to the series combination of $R_s$ and $R_l$ carrying the same current $I$.
