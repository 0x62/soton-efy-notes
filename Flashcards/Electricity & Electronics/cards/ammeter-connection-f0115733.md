## How must an ammeter be connected and what is its ideal internal resistance?

---

Connect an ammeter in series; an ideal ammeter has zero resistance ($R=0$).

Real ammeters have a small resistance and are protected by a fuse. Inserting an ammeter changes circuit current if its resistance is nonzero.
