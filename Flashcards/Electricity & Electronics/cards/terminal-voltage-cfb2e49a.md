## What is the terminal voltage $V$ of a battery in terms of its electromotive force (EMF), internal resistance $r$ and current $I$?

---

$$V = EMF - Ir$$

Terminal voltage is the potential difference across the battery terminals. When $I=0$, $V=EMF$; when current flows the internal resistance $r$ causes a voltage drop $Ir$, so $V<EMF$.
