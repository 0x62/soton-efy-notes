## For two resistors $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ in parallel with total source current $I_{t}$, express the node voltage $V$ in terms of $I_{t},R_{1},R_{2}$.

---

$$V=I_{t}\times\dfrac{R_{1}R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$$

Derivation: $V=I_{t}\big(\dfrac{1}{R_{1}}+\dfrac{1}{R_{2}}\big)^{-1}=I_{t}\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{R_{1}}+\dfrac{1}{R_{2}}}=I_{t}\dfrac{R_{1}R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$.
