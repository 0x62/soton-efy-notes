## How do insulators, conductors and semiconductors differ in terms of valence electrons and electrical behaviour?

---

Insulators: valence electrons tightly bound → resist current. Conductors: valence electrons loosely bound → allow current. Semiconductors: typically 4 valence electrons → behaviour depends on voltage or impurities.

Semiconductors can be doped (add impurities) or have their conductivity changed by applied voltage; this allows devices like diodes and transistors to control current.
