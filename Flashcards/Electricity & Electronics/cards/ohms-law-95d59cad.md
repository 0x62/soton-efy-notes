## State Ohm's Law relating voltage $V$, current $I$ and resistance $R$.

---

$$V=IR$$

Rearrangements: $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$ and $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$. Ohm's Law gives the linear relationship between voltage, current and resistance for many circuit elements; the formula book lists $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$.
