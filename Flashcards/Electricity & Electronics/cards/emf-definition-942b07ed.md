## What is electromotive force (EMF) and how is it measured?

---

EMF is the open-circuit potential of an energy source (the maximum voltage it can provide when no current flows). It is measured in volts (V).

EMF is not a force; it is a potential difference produced by a source (battery/generator). Under load, internal resistance causes terminal voltage to be lower than the EMF.
