## What is ionisation and how does it change an atom's charge?

---

Ionisation is the process of an atom losing or gaining electrons; losing electrons makes the atom positively charged, gaining electrons makes it negatively charged.

Ionisation occurs when enough energy is supplied to remove (or add) electrons from the valence shell. The resulting charged particle is called an ion.
