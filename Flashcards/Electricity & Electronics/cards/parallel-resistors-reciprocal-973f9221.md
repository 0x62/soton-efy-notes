## What is the formula for the equivalent resistance of resistors in parallel?

$\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}$

---

$$\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}$$

Resistors in parallel share the same voltage; currents through each resistor add, so the reciprocals of the resistances sum to give the reciprocal of the equivalent resistance. This extends to any number of parallel branches.
