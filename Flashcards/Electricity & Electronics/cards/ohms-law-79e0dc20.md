## State Ohm's Law and the rearranged forms for $I$ and $R$.

---

$V=I\,R$

Rearrangements: $$I=\dfrac{V}{R}$$ $$R=\dfrac{V}{I}$$

Ohm's Law relates voltage $V$ (volts), current $I$ (amperes) and resistance $R$ (ohms). Use the form needed to solve for the unknown quantity.
