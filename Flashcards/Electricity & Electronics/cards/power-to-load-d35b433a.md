## What is the power delivered to a load $R_l$ by a source with EMF $V$ and source resistance $R_s$?

---

$$P = \dfrac{V^2 R_l}{(R_s + R_l)^2}$$

Derivation:

1. Current: $I = \dfrac{V}{R_s+R_l}$
2. Load power: $P = I^2 R_l = \left(\dfrac{V}{R_s+R_l}\right)^2 R_l = \dfrac{V^2 R_l}{(R_s+R_l)^2}$.
