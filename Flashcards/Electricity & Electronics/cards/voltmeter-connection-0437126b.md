## How must a voltmeter be connected and what is its ideal internal resistance?

---

Connect a voltmeter in parallel with the element; an ideal voltmeter has infinite resistance ($R=\infty$).

A real voltmeter's finite internal resistance forms a parallel path and lowers the effective resistance seen, which can alter voltages (voltmeter loading).
