## What are the equivalent formulas for electrical power?

---

$$P = V I\qquad P = I^2 R\qquad P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Derivation:

Start from $P=VI$. Using $V=IR$ gives $P=I(IR)=I^2R$. Using $I=V/R$ gives $P=V(V/R)=V^2/R$.

- $P$ = power (watts, W)
- $V$ = voltage (V)
- $I$ = current (A)
- $R$ = resistance (\Omega)
