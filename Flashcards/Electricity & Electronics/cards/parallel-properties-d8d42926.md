## For resistors in parallel, which electrical quantities are the same across every resistor and which vary?

---

The voltage is the same across every resistor; the currents through each branch vary and sum to the total current.

Each branch current is $I_{Ri}=\dfrac{V}{R_{i}}$ and $I_{t}=\sum I_{Ri}$.
