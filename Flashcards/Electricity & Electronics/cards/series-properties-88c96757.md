## For resistors in series, which electrical quantities are the same across every resistor and which vary?

---

The current is the same through every resistor; the voltage across each resistor varies.

Because there is only one path, $I$ is common. Voltage drops obey $V_{Ri}=I\,R_{i}$ and sum to the supply: $V_{s}=\sum V_{Ri}$.
