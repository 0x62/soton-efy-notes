## What is a short circuit and why is it dangerous?

---

A short circuit is an unintended low-resistance path between two points that should be separate, causing a large current to flow.

Excessive current generates heat which can damage components, melt insulation and start fires. Protective devices (fuses, circuit breakers) are used to interrupt large fault currents.
