## State the basic electrical power formula and the derived forms using Ohm's Law.

---

$P=I\times V$

Using $V=I\,R$ gives: $$P=I^2\,R$$ Using $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$ gives: $$P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$$

These forms let you compute power when different pairs of quantities are known: $P$ in watts, $I$ in amperes, $V$ in volts, $R$ in ohms.
