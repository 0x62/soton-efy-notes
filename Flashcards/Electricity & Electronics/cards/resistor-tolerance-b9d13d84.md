## A $100\,\Omega$ resistor has a $\pm5\%$ tolerance. What is the possible resistance range?

---

$$[95\,\Omega,\;105\,\Omega]$$

Calculation: $100\times(1\pm0.05)=100\times[0.95,1.05]=[95,105]\,\Omega$. Tolerance specifies manufacturing variation.
