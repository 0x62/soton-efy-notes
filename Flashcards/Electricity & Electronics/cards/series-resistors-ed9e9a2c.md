## What is the equivalent resistance of resistors in series?

$R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$

---

$$R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$$

Resistors in series share the same current; voltages across each resistor add, so the total resistance is the sum of individual resistances. This generalises to any number of series resistors.
