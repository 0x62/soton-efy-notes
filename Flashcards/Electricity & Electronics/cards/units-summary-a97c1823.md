## State these unit definitions: $1\ A$, $1\ V$, $1\ \Omega$, $1\ J$.

---

$1\ A=1\ \mathrm{C}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$

$1\ V=1\ \mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}$

$1\ \Omega=1\ \dfrac{V}{A}$

$1\ J=1\ \mathrm{N\cdot m}$

These define the SI relationships used throughout circuit calculations.
