## Define voltage and give its relationship to energy and charge.

---

$V=\dfrac{E}{Q}$

Voltage (electrical potential difference) is energy per unit charge. One volt equals one joule per coulomb: $1\ V=\dfrac{1\ J}{1\ C}$.
