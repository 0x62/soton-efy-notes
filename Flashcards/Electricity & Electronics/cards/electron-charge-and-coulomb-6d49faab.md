## What is the charge of a single electron and how many electrons make one coulomb?

---

Single electron charge: $-1.6\times 10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$. Number of electrons in one coulomb: $6.25\times 10^{18}$ electrons.

The sign indicates electrons carry negative charge. One coulomb is a large number of elementary charges: $1\ \mathrm{C}=\dfrac{1}{1.6\times10^{-19}}\approx6.25\times10^{18}$ electrons.
