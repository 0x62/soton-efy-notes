## What are the relations $I = n A v e$ and $J = \frac{I}{A}$ and what do the symbols mean?

$I = n A v e\qquad J = \frac{I}{A}$

---

$$I = n A v e\qquad J = \frac{I}{A} = n v e$$

- $I$ = current (A)
- $n$ = charge carrier number density (carriers per m$^3$)
- $A$ = cross-sectional area (m$^2$)
- $v$ = drift velocity of carriers (m/s)
- $e$ = charge per carrier (C), e.g. electron charge $\approx 1.602\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$
- $J$ = current density (A/m$^2$); from $J=I/A$ we get $J=n e v$
