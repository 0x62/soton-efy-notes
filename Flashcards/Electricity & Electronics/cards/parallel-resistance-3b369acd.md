## Formula for total resistance $R_{t}$ of resistors in parallel.

---

$$\dfrac{1}{R_{t}}=\dfrac{1}{R_{1}}+\dfrac{1}{R_{2}}+\dfrac{1}{R_{3}}+\dots$$

Therefore $$R_{t}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{R_{1}}+\dfrac{1}{R_{2}}+\dots}.$$ Total parallel resistance is always less than the smallest branch resistance.
