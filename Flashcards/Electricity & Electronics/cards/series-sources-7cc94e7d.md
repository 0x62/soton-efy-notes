## How do EMFs and internal resistances combine when voltage sources are connected in series?

---

EMFs sum algebraically and internal resistances add: total EMF = sum of EMFs, total internal resistance = sum of $r$'s.

Thus series connection increases both net voltage and net internal resistance (which affects terminal voltage under load).
