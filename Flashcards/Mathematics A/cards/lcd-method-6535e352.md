## What is the LCD method for simplifying compound fractions and how is it applied?

---

Multiply numerator and denominator by the least common denominator (LCD) of all small denominators to clear nested fractions.

Steps: identify the LCD (e.g. $xy$ for denominators $x$ and $y$), multiply top and bottom by the LCD, simplify each term and then cancel any common factors. Example:

$$\frac{\frac{1}{x}+\frac{2}{y}}{\frac{3}{x}-\frac{1}{y}}\cdot\frac{xy}{xy}=\frac{y+2x}{3y-x}.$$
