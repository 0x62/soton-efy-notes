## Using $u=\frac{1}{x}$, solve $\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{3}{x}+2=0$ for $x$.

---

$u^2+3u+2=0\Rightarrow u=-1,-2\Rightarrow x=-1,-\tfrac{1}{2}$

Let $u=\frac{1}{x}$. Then

$$\frac{1}{x^2}+\frac{3}{x}+2= u^2+3u+2=0$$

Factor: $$(u+1)(u+2)=0\implies u=-1,-2$. Finally $x=\frac{1}{u}$ gives $x=-1,-\tfrac{1}{2}$.
