## Simplify $\frac{1}{a+\frac{1}{b}}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{b}{ab+1}$

Combine terms in the denominator into a single fraction and invert:

$$a+\frac{1}{b}=\frac{ab+1}{b}$$

$$\frac{1}{a+\frac{1}{b}}=\frac{1}{\frac{ab+1}{b}}=\frac{b}{ab+1}.$$
