## Simplify $\frac{\frac{1}{x}}{\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{yz}{x(y+z)}$

Combine the denominator and multiply by the reciprocal:

$$\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=\frac{y+z}{yz}$$

$$\frac{\frac{1}{x}}{\frac{y+z}{yz}}=\frac{1}{x}\cdot\frac{yz}{y+z}=\frac{yz}{x(y+z)}.$$
