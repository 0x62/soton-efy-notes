## Show what $\frac{2}{\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}}$ simplifies to and name it.

---

$\displaystyle \frac{2xy}{x+y}\text{ (the harmonic mean of }x\text{ and }y\text{)}$

Compute the reciprocal average:

$$\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{x+y}{xy}\implies \frac{2}{\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}}=\frac{2}{\frac{x+y}{xy}}=\frac{2xy}{x+y}.$$
