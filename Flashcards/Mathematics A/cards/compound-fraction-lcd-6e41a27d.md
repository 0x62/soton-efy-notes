## Simplify $\frac{\frac{1}{x}+\frac{2}{y}}{\frac{3}{x}-\frac{1}{y}}$ by clearing nested fractions.

---

$\displaystyle \frac{y+2x}{3y-x}$

Multiply numerator and denominator by the LCD $xy$:

$$\frac{\frac{1}{x}+\frac{2}{y}}{\frac{3}{x}-\frac{1}{y}}\cdot\frac{xy}{xy}=\frac{y+2x}{3y-x}.$$
