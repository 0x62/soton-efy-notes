## Compute $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{yz+xz+xy}{xyz}$

Use common denominator $xyz$ and add the numerators:

$$\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=\frac{yz}{xyz}+\frac{xz}{xyz}+\frac{xy}{xyz}=\frac{yz+xz+xy}{xyz}.$$
