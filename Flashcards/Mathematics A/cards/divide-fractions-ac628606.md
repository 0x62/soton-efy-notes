## Simplify $\frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{ad}{bc}$

Divide by a fraction by multiplying by its reciprocal:

$$\frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}}=\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{d}{c}=\frac{ad}{bc}.$$
