## Express $\frac{1}{(x-a)(x-b)}$ as partial fractions.

---

$\displaystyle \frac{1}{(x-a)(x-b)}=\frac{1}{b-a}\left(\frac{1}{x-a}-\frac{1}{x-b}\right)$

Verify by combining the right-hand side:

$$\frac{1}{x-a}-\frac{1}{x-b}=\frac{(x-b)-(x-a)}{(x-a)(x-b)}=\frac{b-a}{(x-a)(x-b)}$$

Divide by $b-a$ to get the original fraction.
