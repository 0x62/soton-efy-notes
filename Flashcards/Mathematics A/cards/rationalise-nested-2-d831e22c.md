## Simplify $\frac{1}{a+\frac{b}{c}}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{c}{ac+b}$

Combine and invert:

$$a+\frac{b}{c}=\frac{ac+b}{c}$$

$$\frac{1}{a+\frac{b}{c}}=\frac{1}{\frac{ac+b}{c}}=\frac{c}{ac+b}.$$
