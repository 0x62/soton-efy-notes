## Solve $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}=k$ for $x$ in terms of $y$ and $k$.

---

$\displaystyle x=\frac{y}{ky-1}$

Combine the left side and rearrange:

$$\frac{y+x}{xy}=k\implies y+x=kxy\implies x(ky-1)=y\implies x=\frac{y}{ky-1}.$$
