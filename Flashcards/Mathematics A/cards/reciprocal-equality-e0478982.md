## What does $\frac{1}{x}=\frac{1}{y}$ imply about $x$ and $y$?

---

$x = y$

Multiply both sides by $xy$ (assuming $x,y\neq0$) to get equality of the originals:

$$\frac{1}{x}=\frac{1}{y}\implies y= x.$$
