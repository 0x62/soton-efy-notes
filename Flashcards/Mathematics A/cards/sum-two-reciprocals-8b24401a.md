## Compute $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}$

---

$\displaystyle \frac{x+y}{xy}$

Find a common denominator $xy$ and combine:

$$\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{y}{xy}+\frac{x}{xy}=\frac{x+y}{xy}.$$
