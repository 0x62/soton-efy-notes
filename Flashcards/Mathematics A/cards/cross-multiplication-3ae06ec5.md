## Given $\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}$ what equation relates $a,b,c,d$?

---

$ad=bc$

Cross-multiply (assuming $b,d\neq0$):

$$\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}\implies a\cdot d=b\cdot c\implies ad=bc.$$
