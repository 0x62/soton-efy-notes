## What is the SI unit and symbol for electrical resistance?

---

Ohm, symbol $\Omega$ (property symbol $R$)


Resistance values are commonly expressed as $\Omega$, $\text{k}\Omega$, or $\text{M}\Omega$ depending on magnitude.
