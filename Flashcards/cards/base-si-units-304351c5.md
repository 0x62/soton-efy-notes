## What are the base SI units relevant to electrical engineering and their symbols?

---

- Meter: $\text{m}$ (length)
- Kilogram: $\text{kg}$ (mass)
- Second: $\text{s}$ (time)
- Ampere: $\text{A}$ (electric current)
- Kelvin: $\text{K}$ (temperature)


These are the fundamental SI base units used to derive electrical units (e.g., volts, ohms) in engineering contexts.
