## Convert $1\ \text{nF}$ and $1\ \mu\text{F}$ to farads.

---

$1\ \text{nF} = 10^{-9}\ \text{F} = 0.000000001\ \text{F}$


$1\ \mu\text{F} = 10^{-6}\ \text{F} = 0.000001\ \text{F}$


Nano ($\text{n}$) is $10^{-9}$ and micro ($\mu$) is $10^{-6}$; these are common for digital and power-supply capacitors respectively.
