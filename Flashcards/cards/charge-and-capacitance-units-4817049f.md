## What are the SI units and symbols for charge and capacitance?

---

Charge: coulomb, symbol $\text{C}$ (property symbol $Q$)
Capacitance: farad, symbol $\text{F}$ (property symbol $C$)


Note the symbol overlap: charge is usually denoted $Q$ (units C) and capacitance is $C$ (units F). Capacitance relates charge and voltage by $Q=C\,V$.
