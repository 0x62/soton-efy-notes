## What are typical SI prefix ranges for voltage and resistance in electrical engineering?

---

Volt ranges: $\text{kV}$ (high-voltage) down to $\text{mV}$ (small signals).
Resistance ranges: $\text{M}\Omega$ (insulators), $\text{k}\Omega$ (common resistors), $\Omega$ and $\text{m}\Omega$ (busbars/low-resistance conductors).


These typical ranges help spot unrealistic values (e.g., $100\ \text{MV}$ across a lab resistor indicates an error).
