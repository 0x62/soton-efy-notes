## Convert $1\ \text{mA}$ to amperes.

---

$1\ \text{mA} = 10^{-3}\ \text{A} = 0.001\ \text{A}$

The prefix milli ($\text{m}$) means multiply by $10^{-3}$, so multiply milliamps by $10^{-3}$ to get amperes.
