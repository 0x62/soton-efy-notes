## Convert $1\ \mu\text{A}$ to amperes.

---

$1\ \mu\text{A} = 10^{-6}\ \text{A} = 0.000001\ \text{A}$


The prefix micro ($\mu$) means multiply by $10^{-6}$; use this for small sensor currents.
