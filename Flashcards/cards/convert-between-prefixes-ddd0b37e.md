## How do you convert a value between SI prefixes?

---

Determine the power-of-ten difference between prefixes and multiply by $10^{\Delta}$ where $\Delta$ is that difference.


Example: to convert $5\ \text{k}\Omega$ to ohms, $\Delta=3$ so $5\times10^{3}=5000\ \Omega$. To convert $2\ \text{mA}$ to microamperes, $\Delta=3$ so $2\times10^{3}=2000\ \mu\text{A}$.
