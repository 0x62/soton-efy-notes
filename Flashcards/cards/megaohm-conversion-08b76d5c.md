## Convert $1\ \text{M}\Omega$ to ohms.

---

$1\ \text{M}\Omega = 10^{6}\ \Omega = 1\,000\,000\ \Omega$


Mega ($\text{M}$) is $10^{6}$, commonly used for insulators or very large resistances.
