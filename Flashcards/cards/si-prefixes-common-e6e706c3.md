## List common SI prefixes with their symbols and powers of ten.

---

- Tera: $\text{T}$ = $10^{12}$
- Giga: $\text{G}$ = $10^{9}$
- Mega: $\text{M}$ = $10^{6}$
- Kilo: $\text{k}$ = $10^{3}$
- (base): $-$ = $10^{0}$
- Milli: $\text{m}$ = $10^{-3}$
- Micro: $\mu$ = $10^{-6}$
- Nano: $\text{n}$ = $10^{-9}$
- Pico: $\text{p}$ = $10^{-12}$


Use these prefixes to compactly express very large or very small quantities (e.g., $1\ \text{M}\Omega = 10^{6}\ \Omega$).
