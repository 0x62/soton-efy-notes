## What is the SI unit and symbol for electric current?

---

Ampere, symbol $\text{A}$ (property symbol commonly $I$)

An ampere is the SI base unit for electric current; currents in circuits are typically expressed as $\text{A}$, $\text{mA}$, or $\mu\text{A}$.
