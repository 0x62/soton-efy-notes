## What checks should you perform during the Thinking step?

---

Ask what you're asked to find, whether you understand all words, what information/relationships are given, and whether you need additional facts.

- Confirm the target quantity.
- Verify terminology and definitions used in the problem.
- List explicit information given and relationships/equations available.
- Identify any additional facts or laws you must supply (e.g., kinematics, conservation laws).
