## How do you check your final answer for correctness?

---

Verify units are consistent and the numerical value is reasonable given expected ranges.

- Unit check: ensure the result's units match the physical quantity (e.g., mass in kg, speed in m/s).
- Reasonableness: compare magnitude with typical values or limiting cases to spot errors.
