## List the standard questions to ask when first understanding a problem.

---

- What are you being asked to find?
- Do you understand all the words in the problem?
- What information and/or relationships are you given?
- Will you need other facts/relationships not explicitly given?

- Use these to separate explicit data from implicit assumptions and to plan solution steps.
