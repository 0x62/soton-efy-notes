## Why draw a diagram at the start of a physics problem?

---

A diagram helps identify processes, forces, and quantities to label, which guides selection of equations and units.

- Sketch geometry, forces, directions, and known values.
- Label coordinate axes and magnitudes (e.g., masses, distances, angles).
- Use the sketch to choose relevant equations (e.g., Newton's laws, energy, kinematics).
