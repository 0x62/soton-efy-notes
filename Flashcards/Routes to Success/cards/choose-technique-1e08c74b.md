## How should you decide which technique or equation to use?

---

Identify the physical processes and relationships involved, then select equations that directly link known information to the unknown.

- From your diagram and listed data, pick conservation laws or kinematic/dynamic equations that connect given variables to the target.
- Consider if approximations or alternative methods simplify the solution.
