## What is the difference between explicit and implicit information? Give examples.

---

Explicit information is stated directly; implicit information is implied by the scenario.

- Example explicit: “mass = 70 kg” (given directly).
- Example implicit: “dropped from rest” implies initial speed $v_0 = 0$ (not stated but follows from the wording).
