## 09:00 · Circuits tutorial

**Yoshi Tsuchiya (module schedule).** No separate worksheet is specified for this tutorial. The linked Problem Sheet 9 is the closest preparation: it covers series and parallel RLC resonance, and converting three-phase voltages from line-to-neutral to line-to-line.

- **Read bandwidth from half-power points.** On a series-RLC current plot, look for $I_\mathrm{peak}/\sqrt{2}$, not half the peak current: resistance is fixed and power is proportional to current squared. The distance in hertz between the two crossings is the bandwidth. Divide resonant frequency by bandwidth (in the same units) to obtain $Q$.
- **Distinguish series from parallel resonance.** In series, the inductor and capacitor voltage contributions cancel and the input impedance is smallest. In parallel, their branch currents cancel at the input and the input impedance is largest. Reactive current can still flow in each branch.
- **Subtract the phase-voltage phasors in the stated order.** For example, $V_{AN}=120\angle 0^\circ\,\mathrm{V}$ and $V_{BN}=120\angle-120^\circ\,\mathrm{V}$ give $V_{AB}=V_{AN}-V_{BN}=180+j103.9\,\mathrm{V}=207.8\angle30^\circ\,\mathrm{V}$. Swapping the subscripts reverses that phasor.

**Takeaway:** before using a formula, identify series or parallel, peak or RMS, and line-to-line or line-to-neutral. [Dated schedule](https://moodle.ecs.soton.ac.uk/mod/page/view.php?id=43581) · [Problem Sheet 9](https://moodle.ecs.soton.ac.uk/course/section.php?id=14355).

## 10:00 · C9: Analogue Input

The Il Matto's ADC turns an input voltage into a number. First check that conversion on the potentiometer at **A0**. Then use the reflective optical sensor at **A1** to detect an object, and the filtered pulse signal at **A2** to measure heart rate.

- **Quantisation:** at a 3.3 V reference, one 10-bit step is $3.3/1024\approx 3.22\,\mathrm{mV}$. A count near 512 represents roughly half the reference voltage.
- **Proximity:** compare the voltage with and without a reflecting object. Use a lower threshold to turn the indicator on and a higher threshold to turn it off. Between them, keep the previous state so small fluctuations do not make it flicker.
- **Heart rate:** measure the time between two equivalent crossings of the filtered pulse signal. For example, 40 samples between peaks at 50 samples/s gives $40/50=0.80\,\mathrm{s}$ per beat, or $60/0.80=75$ beats/min.

**Takeaway:** choose thresholds from the measured waveform rather than copying nominal voltages. Keep the ADC's conversion clock distinct from the rate at which the program takes samples.
