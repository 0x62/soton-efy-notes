## Resonance

$$
V_{c}(j\omega) \rightarrow |\frac{w_{n}^2}{w_{n}^2-2^2}| \cos(\omega t)
$$


$$
V_{c}(j\omega) = \frac{w_{n}^2}{w_{n}^2-2^2} V(j \omega)
$$

- No phase shift between input and output impedances
- Only the voltage amplitude changed at the output

Goal: obtain $V_{c}(t)$ by solving the differential equation with KVL

$$
v=v_{L}+v_{C}=L \frac{di}{dt}+v_{C}=LC \frac{d^2v_{c}}{dt^2}+v_{C}
$$

$$
v_{L}=L \frac{di}{dt} \qquad i=C \frac{dv_{C}}{dt}
$$

$$
\frac{d^2v_{C}}{dt^2}+\frac{1}{LC}v_{C}=\frac{1}{LC}v
$$
$$
\frac{d^2v_{C}}{dt^2}+\omega^2_{n}v_{C}=\omega^2_{n}v
$$


Natural frequency or system frequency:

$$
\omega_{n}=\sqrt{ \frac{1}{LC} }
$$
